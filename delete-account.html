Account & data deletion

You can request deletion of your Vita account and all data associated with it at any time. Here’s how.

How to request deletion

  1. Email vatsunp11@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Vita account.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Vita account”.
  3. We verify the request and delete your account and associated data, then confirm by email.
Send your request to
vatsunp11@gmail.com
Tap and hold (or click) to copy · subject: “Delete my Vita account”

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, and account ID).
  • Your gameplay data — guesses, scores, levels, XP, and streaks.

All of the above is permanently removed. We do not retain a copy after deletion, except where required by law.

Timeframe

Requests are processed within 30 days. You’ll receive an email confirmation once your data has been deleted.

Questions

Contact us any time at vatsunp11@gmail.com.
