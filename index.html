Privacy Policy

Last updated · 20 June 2026

Vita is an educational medical-quiz game. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Vita is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis for real patients.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID through Firebase Authentication.
  • Gameplay data — your guesses, answers, scores, levels, XP, and daily streaks, stored so we can show your progress.

We do not collect health records, contacts, precise location, photos, or financial information.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save your progress and run the games.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing & processors

We use Google Firebase (authentication) and our own backend server to store your account and progress. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time — see Delete your account for steps. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Children

Vita is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will appear here with a new “last updated” date.
