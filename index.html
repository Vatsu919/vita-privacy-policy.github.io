Vita · Last updated 20 June 2026

Vita (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is an educational medical-quiz game. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Vita is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis for real patients.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID through Firebase Authentication.
  • Gameplay data — your guesses, answers, scores, levels, XP, and daily streaks, stored so we can show your progress.

We do not collect health records, contacts, precise location, photos, or financial/payment information.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save your progress and operate the games.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing and processors

We use Google Firebase (authentication) and our own backend server to store your account and progress. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email us at vatsunp11@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Children

Vita is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy
